## 20. For High-Stakes Documents, Extract Sourced Facts Before Writing

For a long, high-stakes document, have the AI work like a research assistant: first the facts with links to their sources, then the open questions, and only then the report, built from facts a person has checked.

- **Use when:** a long document will be built from many records, and errors would be costly.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Ask the AI to read every record and pull out the relevant facts, each with a link back to where it came from.
  2. Ask it to list where sources disagree or leave a question open.
  3. Resolve the contradictions and fill the gaps.
  4. Have the AI assemble the final document from the checked facts.
- **Requires:** source documents the AI can read and link to.
