## 47. Ask the AI to Argue the Opposite

Models argue almost any position convincingly, so an argument polished with AI can feel stronger than it is. Ask for the opposite case before relying on it.

- **Use when:** forming a view, writing an argument, or making a decision with AI help.
- **Implementation:**
  1. After working an argument through with the AI, ask it to argue the opposite.
  2. Ask for several directions, not just one.
  3. Watch for sycophancy: the model agreeing with whatever the user seems to want.
