## 27. State the Quality Bar, and Name the Material the AI Must Not Imitate

The AI cannot tell a throwaway draft from work that will be used for years, and it copies the existing material. Say which kind of work it is, and which existing material it must not copy.

- **Use when:** asking for any work that will be reused or published.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Say what kind of work it is, and why, for example: "This is a prototype. Speed over perfection." or "Production code. Must be maintainable."
  2. Tell the AI which existing material or patterns it must not imitate, for example in the always-loaded instruction file.
     > [!info] Sources disagree
     > One source cleans up the material first[^M-newstarter]; another names the patterns not to copy in the instruction file[^ABP].
- **Limits:** a few lines of instruction lose against thousands of lines of contrary examples.
- **Reference:** [^M-ralph11][^M-newstarter][^ABP][^AN-prompt]
