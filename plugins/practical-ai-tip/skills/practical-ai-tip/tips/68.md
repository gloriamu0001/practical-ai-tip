## 68. Vet Tools, Skills and Templates Before Installing Them

Anything an agent loads from outside, such as connectors, skills, plugins and prompt templates, can hide instructions or harmful code. Check it before turning it on.

- **Use when:** adding a connector, skill, plugin or shared prompt from someone else.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Use known, curated sources, and avoid untrusted ones.
  2. Check where an item comes from before installing or activating it.
- **Limits:** a hosted tool can change after approval; pinning, signing and stopping on ambiguous tool names: for builders of agent systems only.
