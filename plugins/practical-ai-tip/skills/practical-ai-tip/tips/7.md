## 7. Bring Big, Ambitious Problems

The value gained from AI grows with the size and difficulty of the problems handed to it. A question with a one-paragraph answer barely tests it.

- **Use when:** choosing what to work on with AI.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Keep a list of large, difficult problems that matter.
  2. Give current models more ambitious tasks than feel comfortable, and let them run.
  3. To learn what a new model can do, give it an open-ended task with a large token budget and see how far it gets.
  4. Keep reading critical output.
- **Limits:** ambitious runs make more decisions alone; models check visual and interactive output poorly.
- **Requires:** a current model; budget for long runs.
