## 15. Use the Largest Model for Idea Work, a Smaller One for Execution

Idea work depends on what the model learned in training; executing a detailed plan depends mostly on the material given in the session.

- **Use when:** choosing a model for planning versus doing.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Use the largest available model for interviews, planning and brainstorming, where suggestions must come from the model itself.
  2. Use a smaller, cheaper model to execute a detailed plan.
  3. Before switching, compare the total cost on a small test.
- **Limits:** small models need a precise plan; hard or ambiguous problems need a larger model; retries can make a cheaper model cost more.
- **Requires:** a tool that allows choosing the model.
