## 62. Use AI to Learn, Not Only to Produce

Ask the AI to explain the reasons behind its work, to show them visually, and to test the user's understanding.

- **Use when:** working in an unfamiliar area, or when the result must be understood, not only used.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Switch on an explaining output style: in Claude Code, `/config` offers "Explanatory" (explains as it works) and "Learning" (coaches the user through changes).
  2. Ask for visual explanations as HTML pages or diagrams.
  3. Explain the subject back to the AI, and let it ask follow-up questions that expose gaps.
  4. Ask for a quiz on the material.
  5. Ask why the AI chose its approach over a named alternative, and which trade-offs it made.
  6. For work that must be fully understood, have the AI teach it stage by stage as the session goes, not all at once at the end: the user restates first, the AI fills the gaps and quizzes, and the session ends only when understanding is shown.
