## 19. Fence the Sources, and Separate Fact From Inference

Tell the AI which sources it may use and what to do when they conflict or are silent, and have it label what is confirmed, what is inferred and what is missing, instead of filling gaps.

- **Use when:** asking for any summary, brief, report or analysis that others will rely on.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Name the files or sources the AI may use, and say to use only those.
  2. Say what to do when the sources conflict or do not answer.
  3. Require each major claim to cite its source, or to be marked as needing verification.
  4. Ask for confirmed facts, inferences and missing information to be kept separate, with conflicts and unverified details first.
  5. Where information is missing, ask for a placeholder such as "TBD" instead of an invented decision, owner or date.
  6. For broad research, have the findings written into a table that can be reviewed, with the source, its date, the evidence and open questions. Keep promising leads apart from well-supported examples, and state what evidence would promote a lead.
- **Asset:** clauses from the source:
  ```
  Use only the supplied sources. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions or inferences. Mark any statement that requires additional verification.
  Cite the source supporting each major claim. Do not fill gaps with assumptions.
  ```
- **Limits:** labelling does not remove errors.
