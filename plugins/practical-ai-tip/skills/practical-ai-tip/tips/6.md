## 6. Judge AI by Current Paid Tools, and Know Where It Is Strongest

Free and outdated models understate what current AI can do. Capability is also uneven: it is strongest where answers can be checked automatically.

- **Use when:** deciding whether AI can handle a task, or explaining AI capability to others.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Judge capability by the latest paid agentic tools, not by a free chatbot tried months ago.
  2. Expect the biggest gains in work whose results can be checked automatically and that is commercially valuable, such as programming and mathematics. Expect smaller gains in search, writing and advice.
  3. Before relying on a model for a task, ask whether the task is close to what models are trained hard on, or far from it. Check more where it is far.
- **Limits:** the line moves with each model generation.
- **Requires:** a current paid AI tool.
