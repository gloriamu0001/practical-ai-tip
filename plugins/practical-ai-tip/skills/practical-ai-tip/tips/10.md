## 10. Have the AI Restate the Goal Before It Starts

Before the AI starts work, ask it to restate the goal and the problem in its own words, so misunderstandings show up before any work is done.

- **Use when:** handing the AI a request, a report or a long message thread to act on.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Hand over the material: the request, the report or the thread.
  2. Before anything else, ask the AI to restate in its own words, in plain language, what it thinks the goals are and what problem needs solving.
  3. Correct any misunderstanding, or any side issue it has fixed on, before work starts.
  4. Leave out guesses about the cause, so the restatement shows the AI's own understanding.
- **Asset:** the source's prompt:
  ```
  restate in your own words what you think my goals are and what the problem i'm trying to solve is
  ```
