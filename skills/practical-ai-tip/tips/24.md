## 24. Ask for the Outline Before the Deliverable

Before the AI builds a deck, document or site, ask for a short written outline of the story, edit it, and only then build.

- **Use when:** asking for any substantial deliverable.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Give the source material, the audience, the purpose and the decision wanted.
  2. Ask for a concise outline first: the bottom line, what changed, why it matters, the evidence, the options, the risks and open questions, and the next step.
  3. Edit the outline: move sections, challenge assumptions, remove what the audience already knows, add the question the audience will certainly ask.
  4. Then have the AI build the deliverable.
- **Asset:** the source's prompt:
  ```
  Review the approved report, spreadsheet, and previous presentation attached.
  I need to brief [audience] on [topic]. The purpose of the briefing is to [inform, align, recommend, or secure a decision].
  Before creating slides, propose a concise narrative with:
  The bottom line
  What changed
  Why it matters
  The evidence
  Available options
  Risks and unresolved questions
  The decision or next step
  Use only the attached sources. Clearly identify any information that is missing or uncertain.
  ```
- **Limits:** the deliverable can still contain errors.
