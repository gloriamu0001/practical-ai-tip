## 2. Delegate Like Briefing a Colleague

Current models work best when treated like a capable colleague who receives a clear brief, not like a pair partner steered line by line.

- **Use when:** handing a task of more than a few minutes to an agent.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Write a clear brief: the goal, the constraints, how success will be checked, how much leeway the agent has, and when it must come back for help.
  2. Hand over the raw material (a message thread, a log, a document) and the goal. Leave the method to the agent. Attach a chart, screenshot or diagram as an image rather than retyping its numbers.
  3. Do by hand only the steps that need a person, such as creating accounts, paying, or obtaining access keys. Hand everything else to the agent.
  4. Return when the agent finishes or asks a real question.
  5. Treat many questions or drift as a sign that the brief was incomplete. Improve the brief, not the agent.
- **Asset:** the source's questions for checking a brief:
  ```
  Can you say what you want & how much leeway the AI has? Can you specify what you want tested & when to come to you for help? Can you describe what good looks like?
  ```
- **Limits:** only for work that can be checked; not for quick exploration.
