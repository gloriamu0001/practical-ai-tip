## 48. Ask the AI for a Critique, Not a Rewrite

Before a draft goes to formal review, have the AI act as a critic and give a prioritised critique first, not a rewrite.

- **Use when:** a draft is about to be reviewed, presented or sent.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Give the draft, and ask the AI to act as a critic.
  2. Ask it to name the claims it would challenge, the weak arguments, the evidence that appears incomplete or unsupported, the assumptions not stated, and the options or trade-offs that are missing.
  3. Ask for a prioritised critique first, without a rewrite.
  4. For a draft the AI wrote, also ask it to check for unsupported claims, invented details and commitments nobody approved, and to list what a person must verify.
  5. Fix the confirmed issues.
- **Asset:** an official source's review prompt for an AI-written draft, without its closing request for a revised version:
  ```
  Check this draft for unsupported claims, invented details and commitments I have not approved. Identify anything a person needs to verify. Do not send or publish anything.
  ```
- **Limits:** does not replace legal, policy, technical or leadership review.
