## 59. Split Long Documents When the Answer Grows With Them

When the answer grows with the document, such as a summary of each section or a list of every complaint, split the document into pieces, answer piece by piece, then combine the results.

- **Use when:** asking the AI to summarise or list items across a long document.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Split the document at natural boundaries, such as sections or chapters.
  2. Process each piece separately, then combine the results.
  3. Test the split on the actual material, even for a document that fits in the context window.
- **Limits:** no rule of thumb fits every case.
