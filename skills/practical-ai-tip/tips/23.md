## 23. Ask for Plans Led by the Decisions Most Likely to Change

A useful plan is short, puts the decisions the user will want to adjust first, and ends with the open questions.

- **Use when:** asking the AI for any plan.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Ask for the decisions most likely to be changed at the top, and the mechanical work at the bottom.
  2. Ask for the plan to be extremely concise.
  3. Ask for a list of unresolved questions at the end.
- **Asset:** standing rules from the source, for an instruction file or the start of a planning request:
  ```
  - Make the plan extremely concise. Sacrifice grammar for the sake of concision.
  - At the end of each plan, give me a list of unresolved questions to answer, if any.
  ```
- **Limits:** very terse plans are hard for others to read.
