## 12. Keep Each Alignment Session Small, and Steer It

A planning conversation on too large a topic hides questions that need a prototype. Split the topic, and steer.

- **Use when:** a planning conversation keeps growing.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Ask the AI first to split a large topic into smaller pieces, and plan each piece in its own session.
  2. Steer actively: hold the direction and cut questions that do not matter yet.
  3. Stop planning when new questions no longer change the plan, and start the work.
- **Limits:** a passive user gets hundreds of questions; an over-eager user keeps planning too long.
