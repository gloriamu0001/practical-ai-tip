## 18. Give the Richest Reference Available

A concrete reference beats a description: a working mockup, a real example of the desired output, existing material to adapt, or a scoring rubric.

- **Use when:** briefing work where the result must match something specific.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Point the AI to a real example of the desired output, or to existing material to adapt: a sample file, a customer message, a screenshot, or the best existing decks in a folder it can reach. Paste the house style guidelines into the request where they exist.
  2. For design, give a working HTML mockup rather than a screenshot or a description.
  3. To encode taste, write a rubric: a short list of what "good" means. An agent can then check results against it.
  4. Give an early draft and its final version together, so the AI can work out the standard from what changed.
  5. Ask the agent to look at earlier work of the same kind first, and to search the web for references.
- **Limits:** with current models, many examples can make the output less creative; matching a house style takes a few rounds.
- **Requires:** an AI that accepts files or links.
