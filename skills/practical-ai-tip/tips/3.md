## 3. Keep Judgement and Understanding; Delegate the Legwork

Hand repetitive work to the AI, and keep the decisions and the understanding of the result.

- **Use when:** deciding what to hand to AI.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Delegate repetitive work: transcription, text generation, moving content from one format into another.
  2. Keep the decisions. Review every output that other people will see.
  3. As agents take over more of the legwork, spend the freed time on oversight: making sure the result is understood well enough to stand behind it.
  4. Read raw outputs personally first, and use AI to scale what was learned, not to avoid looking.
  5. Let the person who knows the domain make the quality calls. Handing the review to someone else breaks the link between seeing a failure and understanding it.
- **Asset:** a line the source cites often:
  ```
  you can outsource your thinking
  but you cannot outsource your understanding
  ```
- **Limits:** reviewing takes time; AI checkers miss whether a result is good or fit for purpose; AI is weaker at choosing which option matters.
