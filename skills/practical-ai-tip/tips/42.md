## 42. Use Pass-or-Fail Checks, Not Rating Scales

A pass-or-fail verdict forces a clear decision and gives more consistent judgements than a 1–5 scale.

- **Use when:** defining how outputs will be judged, by a person or by an AI checker.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Judge each kind of failure with a pass-or-fail verdict.
  2. To track gradual improvement, split a quality into several pass-or-fail checks, for example "4 out of 5 expected facts included".
  3. For one overall number, count an output as passing only if it passes every check, and keep the per-check results.
