## 53. Work on a Copy of Important Files, Then Audit the Changes

Before the AI makes substantial changes to an important file, duplicate it, and afterwards audit what changed.

- **Use when:** the AI will make substantial changes to a deck, document or spreadsheet that matters.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Duplicate the important file before the changes.
  2. After the changes, audit formulas, assumptions, citations and changed cells.
  3. Check facts, numbers, quotations and calculations, and check every chart against its data. As a separate final step, in a fresh session where possible, have the AI check the tables, charts and drafts against the supplied sources. Ask it to list wrong totals, mismatched dates or units and unsupported statements, and to name the exact review a person must do before any action. Then verify its list.
- **Limits:** the AI can misread a source, change text unexpectedly or add an unsupported conclusion.
- **Requires:** an AI that edits files.
