## 16. Speak Instead of Typing

Speaking gets more detail into a prompt faster than typing, and models reconstruct messy speech well.

- **Use when:** the AI needs a lot of context, or ideas come faster than typing.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Switch on voice input or dictation. In Claude Code, run `/voice` and hold the space bar; the desktop app and Cowork have a voice button.
  2. Talk freely for as long as needed, for example about ten minutes. Grammar does not matter.
  3. Optionally say first that the input is dictated, or turn the ramble into a short interview of a few turns.
  4. Read the AI's restatement of the ramble, and correct what is wrong.
- **Limits:** dictation often mishears names, numbers and technical terms.
- **Requires:** voice input or a dictation tool.
