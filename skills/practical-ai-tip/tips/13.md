## 13. Write the Agreed Decisions Down Before Building

At the end of a planning conversation, its decisions are the most valuable thing the session produced. Use them or write them down before clearing.

- **Use when:** a planning conversation ends.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Before clearing the conversation, ask the AI to write a handoff document (a brief or requirements) from it, saved as a file.
  2. Build from that file in a fresh session.
  3. Check the handoff document against the original answers. Summaries soften exact decisions, such as orderings, things that must not happen, and specific numbers.
- **Limits:** a handoff always loses some detail.
