## 52. Let the AI Recommend; Apply Changes Only After Approval

For changes that others will see or that are hard to undo, have the AI give its recommendation with reasons and evidence, then wait for approval.

- **Use when:** the AI would change shared records, such as statuses, closing a request or sending a reply.
- **Implementation:**
  1. Ask the AI to give its recommendation, its reasoning and the evidence it found, then stop.
  2. Apply nothing until a person approves. When approving, name the exact action approved, as in a source's software example "Yes, run the migration on the staging database", not "go" or "continue". To approve many items at once, have the AI collect them in one shared document, approve them by commenting in one pass, then let the AI carry out the approved items.
  3. When working through a list of follow-ups, have the AI first check the original conversation and whether the item is already handled, then bring one decision at a time with a link to the source.
  4. Mark every comment the AI posts as AI-generated.
  5. Treat every send, edit, invitation and share as a proposed action until a person approves it. Before deleting, sending or publishing, have the AI show a preview of the exact change, and confirm. End requests by asking for the result in draft only.
  6. Pause whenever the agent proposes something outside the original task.
- **Asset:** an example prompt from the source, for a to-do list that spans email, chat and calendar:
  ```
  # Work through my list with me
  Use the working list I provide. Go one item at a time.
  For each item, read the linked source conversation and relevant recent replies. If scheduling is involved, compare the current invitation with my calendar. Do not treat an old summary as the current state.
  Before asking me anything, check whether I have already replied, whether someone else owes the next step, and whether the item is just an FYI. Then give me a short explanation and the one decision you need from me, with a link to the source.
  Draft the reply or calendar change when useful. Do not send messages, accept invitations, or make commitments without my authorization for that action.
  After we handle the item, update the list. Distinguish drafted, sent, complete, and waiting on someone else. Record the next step so I do not have to reconstruct the conversation tomorrow.
  Start with the first item that needs my decision.
  ```
- **Limits:** slower than letting the AI act; worth it only for visible or hard-to-undo changes.
- **Requires:** write access, for applying approved changes.
